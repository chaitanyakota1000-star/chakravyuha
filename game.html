Your guide to the battlefield

Rules of the Field

Lead the Pandavas. The computer leads the Kauravas. Use each champion's movement and strength wisely to make the opposing army retreat.

01

Choose a champion

Select Yudhishthira, Bhima, or Arjuna to reveal their available moves and targets.

02

Move, then strike

Each champion may move one square and attack once per round. You can do both with the same champion in the same turn.

03

Watch the colors

Green squares are open moves. Red squares are rivals in range. Select a red square to attack; the battle log shows damage.

04

Use their strengths

Arjuna attacks up to two squares away (3 damage). Bhima deals 3 crushing damage. Yudhishthira deals 2. Champions strike in all 8 directions (including diagonals)!

Victory

Make all three rival champions retreat. Choose Finish turn when you're ready for the computer's Kaurava commanders to act. New battle resets the field.
