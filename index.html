MAHABHARAT
॥ ॐ नमो भगवते वासुदेवाय ॥

Before the war, there was a choice.

An epic of duty, kinship, and the price of dharma.

The Beginning

From a single royal line came two brothers: Dhritarashtra, born without sight, and Pandu, marked young by a curse.

Pandu's five sons — the Pandavas — grew up beside Dhritarashtra's hundred — the Kauravas. Cousins by blood. Rivals by birthright.

॥ यतो धर्मस्ततो जयः ॥

Those Who Shaped the War

Explore the champions, sovereigns, and divine architects of Kurukshetra.

  • Divine • योगेश्वर 01

    Sri Krishna

    The Divine Charioteer • Yogeshwara

    The supreme cosmic consciousness who entered the mortal war without weapon in hand, guiding Arjuna across the abyss of despair to uphold cosmic Dharma.

    Sacred Lineage

    Eighth avatar of Lord Vishnu, born to Devaki & Vasudeva in Mathura, raised in Vrindavan by Yashoda, sovereign ruler of the ocean citadel Dwaraka.

    Divine Arsenal

    The cosmic Sudarshana Chakra, Kaumodaki mace, Panchajanya conch, and all-pervading omniscient cosmic sight (Vishwaroopa).

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Spoke the 700 verses of the Bhagavad Gita on Day 1; steered Arjuna's chariot; shielded the Pandavas through every catastrophic turning point.

    • \n\n
  • Pandava • महारथी 02

    Arjuna

    The Incomparable Archer • Savyasachi

    Foremost disciple of Drona and master of the Gandiva bow. When kinship paralyzed his resolve at Kurukshetra, Krishna's immortal revelation restored his purpose.

    Sacred Lineage

    Third Pandava, begotten by Queen Kunti from Indra (King of Devas); raised in Hastinapur; blessed by Lord Shiva with the celestial Pashupatastra.

    Divine Arsenal

    The celestial Gandiva bow, inexhaustible quivers of Agni, the white-horse chariot, and mastery of all four classes of divine astras.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Brought down the invincible Bhishma on Day 10 behind Shikhandi, avenged Abhimanyu by slaying Jayadratha, and eliminated Karna on Day 17.

    • \n\n
  • Kaurava Ally • दानवीर 03

    Karna

    The Tragic Sun-Son • Radheya

    Born of the Sun God but raised in the dust of charioteers. Bound by undying gratitude to Duryodhana, he chose tragic honor over an easy throne.

    Sacred Lineage

    Firstborn son of Kunti and Surya (Sun God); set adrift in a basket, raised by charioteer Adhiratha; crowned King of Anga by Duryodhana.

    Divine Arsenal

    The sacred Vijaya bow of Parashurama, natural divine armor (Kavacha-Kundala), and the one-time infallible Vasavi Shakti dart.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Supreme Kaurava Commander on Days 16–17; spared four Pandava brothers honoring his vow to Kunti; felled by Arjuna as his chariot wheel sank into earth.

    • \n\n
  • Patriarch • पितामह 04

    Bhishma

    The Grand Patriarch • Gangaputra

    The unyielding pillar of Hastinapur who renounced his crown and took a terrifying vow of lifelong celibacy to honor his father's happiness.

    Sacred Lineage

    Son of King Shantanu and River Goddess Ganga; original name Devavrata; blessed with Iccha-Mrityu (death only by his own chosen will).

    Divine Arsenal

    Mastery of all celestial astras, unmatched archery prowess, and deep wisdom in statecraft, ethics, and Rajadharma.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Supreme Kaurava Commander for Days 1–10; devastated Pandava legions until pinned upon a bed of arrows by Arjuna, waiting until the northern solstice to depart.

    • \n\n
  • Pandava Queen • याज्ञसेनी 05

    Draupadi

    The Fire-Born Empress • Panchali

    Emerged from the roaring flames of a sacred sacrificial altar. Her humiliation in the royal assembly became the moral catalyst that tore an empire apart.

    Sacred Lineage

    Born fully formed from the Putrakameshti Yajna of King Drupada of Panchala; sister of Dhrishtadyumna; empress to the five Pandavas.

    Divine Arsenal

    Inflexible moral conviction, divine spiritual protection directly granted by Krishna, and the fiery tears of unyielding justice.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Refused to tie her unbound hair for 13 years until washed in Dushasana's blood; her sacred vow was the relentless spiritual engine of the war.

    • \n\n
  • Kaurava Leader • अधिपति 06

    Duryodhana

    The Unyielding Sovereign • Suyodhana

    Crown prince of Hastinapur whose absolute conviction of royal birthright and refusal to share five villages summoned eighteen akshauhinis to war.

    Sacred Lineage

    Firstborn of the hundred Kauravas, son of blind King Dhritarashtra and Queen Gandhari; avatar of Kali.

    Divine Arsenal

    The crushing iron mace (Gada), student of Lord Balarama, and an invulnerable adamantine torso hardened by Gandhari's glance.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Commanded the Kaurava coalition; fought through all 18 days until his final epic mace duel against Bhima at Lake Dvaipayana.

    • \n\n
  • Pandava King • धर्मराज 07

    Yudhishthira

    The Embodiment of Dharma • Dharmaraja

    The eldest Pandava whose commitment to truth was tested by devastating dice, forest exile, and the tragic calculus of total war.

    Sacred Lineage

    Eldest Pandava, born to Queen Kunti from Yama / Dharma (God of Justice & Truth); rightful sovereign of Indraprastha.

    Divine Arsenal

    Mastery of the celestial spear, unwavering moral fortitude, and supreme understanding of statecraft and spiritual duty.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Supreme commander of the Pandava coalition; spoke the pivotal elephant stratagem to disarm Drona; slew King Shalya; crowned Emperor.

    • \n\n
  • Pandava • वृकोदर 08

    Bhima

    The Colossus of Strength • Vrikodara

    A mountain of flesh and fury blessed with the strength of 10,000 elephants, living only to defend his family and avenge the dishonor of Draupadi.

    Sacred Lineage

    Second Pandava, born to Queen Kunti from Vayu (the Wind God); gifted supernatural physical power in the serpent realm Nagaloka.

    Divine Arsenal

    The colossal iron mace, devastating bare-handed wrestling, and irresistible physical momentum that shattered elephant legions.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Fulfilled every oath taken in the dice hall: personally slew all 100 Kaurava brothers, tore open Dushasana, and shattered Duryodhana's thighs.

    • \n\n
  • Prodigy • वीर अभिमन्यु 09

    Abhimanyu

    Hero of the Labyrinth • Veera Abhimanyu

    Sixteen-year-old warrior prodigy who breached the impenetrable Chakravyuha alone, fighting six Maharathis simultaneously with a broken chariot wheel.

    Sacred Lineage

    Son of Arjuna and Subhadra (Krishna's sister); reincarnation of Varchas; learned the entrance to the Chakravyuha in his mother's womb.

    Divine Arsenal

    Bowmanship rivaling his father, lightning chariot maneuvers, celestial astras, and the legendary broken chariot wheel.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Breached the 7-tier formation on Day 13; defeated Duryodhana's son Lakshmana; martyred in an unjust encirclement; his son Parikshit saved the dynasty.

    • \n\n
  • Royal Preceptor • द्रोणाचार्य 10

    Dronacharya

    The Royal Preceptor • Master of Astras

    The revered martial master of both dynasties, caught between lifelong obligation to Hastinapur's throne and affection for his Pandava students.

    Sacred Lineage

    Born miraculously from a clay vessel (Drona) through Sage Bharadwaja; educated by Sage Agnivesha; royal preceptor to the Kuru princes.

    Divine Arsenal

    Master of all known divine astras including the Brahmashira, and the greatest battlefield architect of geometric formations.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Kaurava Commander on Days 11–15; designed the impenetrable Chakravyuha; laid down his celestial arms upon hearing of his son's fall.

    • \n\n
  • Chiranjivi • अश्वत्थामा 11

    Ashwatthama

    The Immortal Avenger • Chiranjivi

    Born with a divine gem in his forehead and the wrath of Rudra in his veins, he became the instrument of the war's darkest vengeance.

    Sacred Lineage

    Son of Guru Drona and Kripi; partial avatar of Lord Rudra (Shiva); born with a protective gem embedded in his brow.

    Divine Arsenal

    The apocalyptic Brahmashira and Narayana astras; immortal resilience granted by Lord Shiva.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Final Kaurava commander; executed the devastating midnight strike on the sleeping Pandava camp on the 18th night; cursed by Krishna to wander earth for 3,000 years.

    • \n\n
  • Gandhara King • सौबल 12

    Shakuni

    The Mastermind of Gandhara • Saubala

    The limping prince of Gandhara who turned a pair of ivory dice into weapons more devastating than any celestial astra to destroy the Kuru house.

    Sacred Lineage

    King of Gandhara, brother of Queen Gandhari, and maternal uncle to Duryodhana and the Kauravas.

    Divine Arsenal

    Enchanted loaded dice carved from his father's bones, psychological manipulation, and master political deception.

    Kurukshetra Turning Point

    Architect of the rigged dice hall humiliation and 13-year forest exile; masterminded the anti-Pandava conspiracies; slain by Sahadeva on Day 18.

The Dice Game

Invited to a game he could not refuse, Yudhishthira wagers his brothers, his wealth, and finally himself — and loses everything, throw after throw.

Dragged before the court, Draupadi is offered no protection by law or kin. What happens in that hall will not be forgotten — not by her, not by the Pandavas, not by the throne itself.

Exile

For thirteen years, the Pandavas live as strangers to their own kingdom — first in the forest, then in disguise, waiting.

Draupadi's vow does not fade with the seasons. Neither does Duryodhana's refusal to share even five villages.

The Road to War

Krishna carries one last offer of peace to Hastinapura. Duryodhana will not yield — not even a needlepoint of land.

Both sides muster their armies. What began as a family's quarrel now belongs to a battlefield.

Kurukshetra
